DL3 external `evaluation` consumes serialized ArmResult/OracleAssertion/ValidationReceipt; no imports into src. No independent truth/compiler/calibration/checker/renderer authored by this team. Wrappers explicit not_run absent admitted actual execution. Scorer consumes future authorized expectations; does not generate them.

No src imports evaluation; arms no gen. No implicit discovery of OUTER truth/.thinking. DL1 owns parser seams, DL2 DSL/parser, DL3 external evaluation serialization, DL4 CLI/report. Consumers reconcile after integration.
