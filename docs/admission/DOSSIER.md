# Admission dossier contract

Provide assets with id, source_url, edition, exact path, owner, SHA256, license_expression, license_text_sha256, use_mode, reviewer, decision and reason. Inventory every actual-use runtime, source, reference, template, terminology, original data and public derivative. Include G1-G7 statuses and manifest-bound H/Q approvals from externally appointed identities.

No downloaded locks, signatures, rights approvals or runtime skips are fabricated in this tree. Missing artifacts remain NOT_ADMITTED. Structural dossier checks cannot establish rights or identity and cannot issue GO. B1 skip requires actual authorized runtime evidence; absence of execution is not_run.
